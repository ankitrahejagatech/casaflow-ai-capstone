Known limitations and operating boundaries

  • Scope: The prototype covers one synthetic, English-language City of San Jose residential home-extension project. Remodeling, new construction, commercial work, and other jurisdictions are outside scope.
  • Who may enter information: Modeled authorized contractors or project leads submit project-board transitions and contractor-chat updates. Scripted homeowner questions, selections, and claims enter only as source evidence. The MVP requires the supplied CSV, JSON, and JSONL schemas and does not provide live identity verification or ingest real inboxes and files.
  • Who may view information: The contractor review contains full case evidence and controls. Homeowner visibility is limited to a derived, read-only view of contractor-approved status and homeowner-relevant decisions; contractor-only notes, margins, bids, and internal reasoning are excluded. Live login and role enforcement are not implemented.
  • Images and inspection evidence: Photos and other images are optional supporting evidence only. They are never treated as proof of full-scope completion, physical safety, code compliance, inspection passage, permit acceptance, or professional approval. Inspection results remain synthetic contractor attestations with no City-system or image verification.
  • Escalation and external action: When information is missing, conflicting, high-stakes, or outside policy, CasaFlowAI preserves state, explains the issue, and requires contractor review or refuses the request. A human must route the matter to the appropriate project lead, inspector, engineer, emergency authority, or privacy owner; the prototype does not contact them or connect to email, scheduling, payment, procurement, subcontractor, or municipal systems.
