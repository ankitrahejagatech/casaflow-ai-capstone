# 00 - How To Work With AI

AI should accelerate your thinking. It should not replace your judgment.

## The Loop

Use this loop throughout the capstone:

1. Ask AI for options.
2. Choose a direction.
3. Ask AI to grill the direction.
4. Revise the idea.
5. Write the final decision in your PRD.

## Good AI Use

- Ask for tradeoffs.
- Ask for failure modes.
- Ask for examples.
- Ask for eval cases.
- Ask for a simpler scope.
- Ask the AI to challenge weak thinking.

## Weak AI Use

- Accept the first answer.
- Use vague language.
- Copy claims you do not understand.
- Hide risks.
- Build a generic chatbot.
- Skip evals.

## Starter Prompt

```text
I am working on my Agentic AI Capstone.
Help me think like an AI product manager, not just a builder.
Ask one question at a time. Push me toward a safe, specific, demoable workflow.
```

## When To Ask For A Grill

Ask for a grill before:

- Choosing the project.
- Submitting Discovery.
- Moving from Design to Develop.
- Recording the demo.
- Submitting the final PRD.
