# Agentic AI Capstone Discovery Companion

Welcome. This companion helps you complete the **Discovery** phase of your Agentic AI Capstone.

It does one job:

```text
Help you choose your own safe, specific, demoable agentic AI capstone project and complete the Discovery PRD answers.
```

## Very Important

Northstar Home is an example only.

Do **not** pick Northstar Home as your project unless your instructor explicitly tells you to use it.

Your job is to choose **your own** project.

The companion can use Northstar Home to show what a good answer looks like, but it should guide you toward your own workflow, your own user, and your own capstone idea.

## What This Companion Will Help You Do

By the end, you should have Discovery PRD answers for:

1. User
2. Workflow
3. Trigger
4. Current process
5. Pain points
6. Agent opportunity
7. Synthetic data plan
8. Human boundary
9. Success metric
10. Initial demo idea

Then stop.

Do not move into Design, Develop, or Deploy yet.

## Step-By-Step Setup In Codex

1. Download the companion ZIP.
2. Start a new Codex chat.
3. Attach the ZIP to the chat, or open the unzipped folder as the workspace if Codex gives you that option.
4. Paste the prompt from `STUDENT_START_PROMPT.md`.
5. Answer one question at a time.
6. When the companion gives you the final Discovery PRD answers, paste those answers into your PRD.
7. Stop. Wait for the Design guide before moving forward.

## Start Prompt

Use this exact prompt:

```text
I am starting the Discovery phase of my Agentic AI Capstone.

Read START_HERE.md, AGENTS.md, and 01_DISCOVERY.md.

Help me choose my own capstone project. Do not recommend Northstar Home as my project. Northstar Home is only an example.

Ask me one question at a time. Grill me until I have a safe, specific, demoable agentic workflow.

Then help me write self-contained Discovery PRD answers for:
1. User
2. Workflow
3. Trigger
4. Current process
5. Pain points
6. Agent opportunity
7. Synthetic data plan
8. Human boundary
9. Success metric
10. Initial demo idea

After the Discovery PRD answers are complete, stop and tell me not to move to Design yet.
```

## Files In This Workspace

- `STUDENT_START_PROMPT.md` - the exact prompt to paste into Codex.
- `CODEX_SETUP_GUIDE.md` - step-by-step setup instructions.
- `01_DISCOVERY.md` - the Discovery walkthrough.
- `DISCOVERY_PRD_TEMPLATE.md` - what to write in the PRD.
- `PROJECT_IDEA_GRILL.md` - questions to pressure-test your idea.
- `SYNTHETIC_DATA_STARTER.md` - how to create safe synthetic data.
- `NORTHSTAR_HOME_EXAMPLE.md` - example only, not the default project.
- `AGENTS.md` - instructions for the AI companion.
- `data/` - sample synthetic data for the Northstar example.
- `policies/` - sample policy docs for the Northstar example.
