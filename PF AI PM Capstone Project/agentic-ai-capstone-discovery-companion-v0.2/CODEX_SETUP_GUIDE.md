# Codex Setup Guide

Follow these steps exactly.

## Step 1: Download

Download the companion ZIP from your course.

## Step 2: Open Codex

Start a new Codex chat for the capstone companion.

## Step 3: Add The Companion

Attach the ZIP to the Codex chat.

If Codex gives you an option to open or use the unzipped folder as a workspace, use that option.

## Step 4: Paste The Start Prompt

Open `STUDENT_START_PROMPT.md`.

Copy the prompt.

Paste it into Codex.

## Step 5: Answer One Question At A Time

The companion should ask you one question at a time.

Do not ask it to build your whole capstone immediately.

## Step 6: Finish Discovery

The companion will help you write Discovery PRD answers for:

1. User
2. Workflow
3. Trigger
4. Current process
5. Pain points
6. Agent opportunity
7. Synthetic data plan
8. Human boundary
9. Success metric
10. Initial demo idea

## Step 7: Stop

When Discovery is complete, stop.

Paste the Discovery answers into your PRD.

Wait for the Design guide before moving forward.

## Common Mistake

Do not use Northstar Home as your project just because it appears in the companion.

Northstar Home is an example only.

Your capstone should be your own project.
