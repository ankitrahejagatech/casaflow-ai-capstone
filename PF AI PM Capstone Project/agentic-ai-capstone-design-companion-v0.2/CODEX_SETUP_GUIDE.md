# Codex Setup Guide

Follow these steps exactly.

## Step 1: Download

Download the companion ZIP from your course.

## Step 2: Open Codex

Start a new Codex chat for the capstone companion.

## Step 3: Add The Companion

Attach the ZIP to the Codex chat.

If Codex gives you an option to open or use the unzipped folder as a workspace, use that option.

## Step 4: Paste The Start Prompt

Open `STUDENT_START_PROMPT.md`.

Copy the prompt.

Paste it into Codex.

## Step 5: Paste Your Discovery Answers

The companion will ask for your completed Discovery PRD answers first.

Paste all ten. If any are missing or vague, fix them with the companion before Design begins.

Then answer one question at a time. Do not ask it to build anything. Design happens on paper.

## Step 6: Finish Design

The companion will help you write Design PRD answers for:

1. Agent role
2. Target workflow
3. Agent loop
4. Inputs and context
5. Tools or simulated tools
6. Memory decision
7. Output format
8. Escalation rules
9. Human approval point
10. Initial eval plan

## Step 7: Stop

When Design is complete, stop.

Paste the Design answers into your PRD.

Wait for the Develop guide before building.

## Common Mistake

Do not switch to Northstar Home as your project just because it appears in the companion.

Northstar Home is an example only.

Your capstone should be your own project, carried over from Discovery.
